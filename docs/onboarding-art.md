## Production prompts

### discover.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production illustration for the first page of MyShop, an existing mobile shopping app. Create a premium tactile 3D still life, a sculptural terracotta shopping tote in center with soft round handles, warm ivory over-ear headphones leaning to its left, a small sage green ceramic cup near its lower right and a tiny floating terracotta heart at upper right. Editorial boutique storefront feel, subtly playful yet sophisticated, clay and matte ceramic surfaces with realistic soft studio shadows and ambient occlusion. Warm brand palette terracotta #BB633F, dark cocoa #7A4A2D, ivory #FFF7EE, muted sage #C2C9B5. Orthographic three-quarter view, carefully balanced generous spacing, all objects visible with no crop, cohesive composition occupying central 80% of a square canvas, full object and shadows isolated on a truly transparent background. No words, no letters, no logos, no labels, no UI, no devices, no watermark. High quality polished 3D render.
