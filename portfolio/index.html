01 / PERSONAL

A basket that stays yours

Saved items, addresses, cart and order history stay tied to the signed-in account.

02 / VERIFIED

Checkout with a clear result

Sandbox payments are checked before a demo order is saved. Retrying an invoice avoids duplicate orders.

Room for every screen

Compact layouts, larger text and reduced motion are part of the app’s automated journey checks.
